## 🎵 About Ravio
**Ravio** is a minimalist, high-performance Android music client engineered for audiophiles who want maximum control without the bloat. It features an in-built AI curator, studio-grade digital signal processing (DSP), real-time synced lyrics, and complete Material 3 customization. 

Zero ads. No subscriptions. No algorithms tracking your every move.

## ✨ Core Features
* 🤖 **Ravio Brain AI:** Built-in intelligence that creates custom taste mixes, Vibe Summaries, Song DNA backstories, and instant live lyrics translations.
* 🎚️ **Dual 10-Band EQ:** Studio-grade multi-band equalizer with DSP bass boost, loudness enhancer, 3D virtualizer, pitch/tempo shift, and silence skipping.
* 📥 **Zero-Auth Playlist Import:** Paste any public **Spotify** or **YouTube / YouTube Music** playlist URL to instantly pull all tracks into your local library without needing an account login.
* 🎤 **Time-Synced Lyrics:** Sing along with word-by-word and line-synced lyrics with beautiful canvas backgrounds.
* 🎨 **Unlimited Customization:** Pure AMOLED black mode, Dynamic Material You theming, customizable player layouts, and blurred album art backdrops.
* 📂 **Offline & Local Playback:** Cache streams for offline listening, manage local device audio files seamlessly, or control your queue via Android Auto.

## 📥 How to Install
1. Go to the [**Official Website**](https://raviomusic.github.io) or the [**Releases Tab**](https://github.com/raviomusic/raviomusic.github.io/releases/latest).
2. Download the latest `Ravio.apk` (v1.1.1).
3. Open the file on your Android device and tap **Install** (if prompted, allow installs from unknown sources in your browser settings).
